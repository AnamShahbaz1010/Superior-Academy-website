Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from prospective students.

What courses does Superior Academy offer?

We offer preparation programs for ISSB, MDCAT, ECAT, and CSS exams, each led by experienced instructors with structured study plans.

How do I enroll in a course?

Click "Login / Register" in the top navigation bar to create an account, then browse our Courses page to enroll in your desired program.

Are classes held online or in-person?

We offer both online and in-person classes depending on the course. Check each course's detail page for specific format information.

Do you provide mock tests and practice material?

Yes, all our programs include regular mock tests, practice questions, and study material designed to closely mirror the actual exams.
